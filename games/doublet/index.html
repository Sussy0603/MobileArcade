How to play

Swipe anywhere on the board. Every tile slides as far as it can in that direction.

Two tiles with the same number touch and become one tile worth double. A tile that has just merged rests until your next move.

After every move that changes the board, one new tile appears — usually a 2, sometimes a 4.

Reach 2048 to win. You can carry on afterwards for as long as there is room.

The game ends when no tile can move and no two neighbours match.

Handy things

Undo: 3 free per game. Need more? Watch a short ad.

Hint shows an arrow for the best next swipe.

Second chance: when the board locks up, watch a short ad and the smallest tiles disappear.

Arrow keys, WASD, U to undo, R for a new game.

Boards come in four sizes. Bigger boards are roomier but slower to build a big tile on.

A working tactic

Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile there. Only use the two directions that keep it pinned, and save the third for emergencies.

Keep your top row ordered from largest to smallest. Merges then cascade instead of scattering.
